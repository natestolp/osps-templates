# OSPS Templates

Reusable security and governance policy templates for open source projects.
They support implementation of the [OSPS Baseline](https://baseline.openssf.org)
by taking the guesswork out of documenting security practices: copy a template,
adapt it to your project, link to the practices you already have in place, and
adopt recommended practices where gaps exist.

Templates beyond the scope of the Baseline may be added over time to support
other efforts from [ORBIT SIG Definitions](https://github.com/ossf/orbit-definitions).
Areas under consideration include cybersecurity policy requirements associated
with the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA).

## Templates

All templates live in the [`templates/`](templates/) directory. Use only the
ones relevant to your project; you are not expected to adopt all of them.

* [Software Composition Analysis (Dependency) Policy](templates/Software-Composition-Analysis-Policy.md)
* [Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD) Policy](templates/Coordinated-Vulnerability-Disclosure-Policy.md)
* [Static Application Security Testing (SAST) Policy](templates/Static-Application-Security-Testing-Policy.md)
* [Test Coverage Policy for Major Changes](templates/Test-Coverage-Policy.md)
* [Secrets and Credentials Management Policy](templates/Secrets%20andCredentials-Management-Policy.md)
* [Escalated Permissions Review Policy](templates/Escalated-Permissions-Review-Policy.md)
